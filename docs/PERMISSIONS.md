## Notes

- **Cost visibility:** `product.cost` controls whether purchase price, stock value, COGS and profit columns are returned. Cashiers and inventory managers never receive them.
- **Discount authority:** cashiers may give manual discounts up to the shop limit (default 5%, per-user override possible). `discount.override` lifts the limit.
- **Credit sales** need `sale.credit`, a registered customer and available credit limit.
- Only a **Super Admin** can assign or modify the Super Admin role; `role.manage` and `backup.manage` are Super-Admin-only by default.
- Custom roles can be created under Settings → Roles & permissions.
